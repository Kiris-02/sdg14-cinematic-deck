TARGET 14.3 QUESTION 1: TARGET AIM Speaker 5

Target 14.3: Minimize Ocean Acidification

Confronting the Severe Chemical Cost of Planetary Carbon Absorption

CARBON BUFFER
25% CO2 Sunk
Oceans absorb 25% of all anthropogenic carbon emissions, creating carbonic acid (H2CO3).
ACIDITY SURGE
+30% Acidity
A 0.1 unit drop in average ocean surface pH represents a 30% surge in hydrogen ions (H+).
CALCIFICATION THREAT
Corrosive Waters
Depletion of carbonate ions (CO3²⁻) dissolves shells of pteropods, mollusks, and corals.
